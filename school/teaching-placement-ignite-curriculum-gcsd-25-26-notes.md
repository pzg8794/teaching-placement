## ♿ SWD Considerations Sheet Capture

- **Use Clear, Simple Language:** Speak slowly and use short sentences. Repeat key points and check for understanding.
- **Incorporate Visual Supports:** Use pictures, icons, schedules, and gestures to support directions and expectations.
- **Establish Predictable Routines:** Keep daily activities consistent. Use visual schedules and countdowns to signal transitions. Consider keeping structure of every class the same. For example: 1. Greeting/Feelings Check In. 2. A song, video, or story for engagement. 3. Lesson activity. 4. Choice activity time.
- **Break Tasks into Small Steps:** Divide activities into manageable parts. Provide step-by-step instructions.
- **Provide Hands-On, Engaging Activities:** Provide hands-on, engaging materials that students can touch and manipulate.Provide accessible materials tailored to students’ abilities and sensory needs, reach out to Special Ed TOSA/Assistive Tech for additional support.
- **Offer Choices and Allow Extra Processing Time:** Give options when possible and wait patiently for student responses.
- **Use Positive Reinforcement:** Praise effort and cooperation frequently. Use tangible rewards if appropriate.
- **Model Expected Behaviors:** Model expected behaviors, including social skills and task procedures.
- **Be Flexible and Patient:** Adjust pacing and supports based on individual student needs.
- **Eliminate Downtime & Have Backup Plans:** Plan extra activities and backup tasks for different ability levels to fill gaps or early finishes. Use quick brain or movement breaks, and switch smoothly between tasks as needed. Reducing downtime helps keep students engaged and focused.
- **Collaborate with Special Education Staff:** Communicate regularly to align strategies and support student success. Classroom teachers will support any individual plans (behavior, communication, etc. Special Education TOSA can support curriculum adaptations, co-planning, modeling etc.
- **Collaborate closely with teaching assistants and aides:** Clarify their roles and plan how they support students and use time effectively in class.
