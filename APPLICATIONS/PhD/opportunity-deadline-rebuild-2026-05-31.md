## Operating rule going forward

European doctoral opportunities must be tracked as two different systems:

1. **Job-style doctoral vacancies**: Sweden, Norway, Germany, Netherlands, Switzerland, and other European universities often post funded PhD roles as jobs with short individual deadlines.
2. **Fellowship / host-affiliation routes**: Fulbright and similar research awards require a country strategy, host outreach, and affiliation letters before the formal deadline.

Do not treat Europe like one annual U.S.-style PhD cycle.
