# Email draft — tomorrow's marketing starter

**To:** SBTE  
**Subject:** Tomorrow's marketing starter: connecting vocabulary to today's examples

Hi,

For tomorrow's opening, I prepared a five-minute vocabulary connection based on today's phone/customer-experience discussion. The starter uses familiar examples, visible vocabulary with plain-language clues, a sentence frame, private rehearsal, and several response options. Students will:

1. Choose one company or customer experience we heard today.
2. Name one customer need or want. (A benefit is the result; it is not the vocabulary term we are practicing.)
3. Use the visible word bank to choose a marketing function. The terms remain separate: marketing information management, product/service management, pricing, promotion, selling, and channel management.
4. Complete the sentence: “Our company or experience is ____. The customer needs ____. The marketing concept is to satisfy that need and make money. ____ helps because the company can ____.”

I will ask a leading question—“What would the company actually do?”—and take two or three quick shares before stopping at five minutes. Students can quietly rehearse, speak, point to a term, write keywords, or partner-share. If they are unsure, they can compare two terms or ask for a clue. This keeps the starter brief, gives students access to the terms, and lets them generate the connection before I add an example or clarification. I will also scan the room and use a planted position rather than pacing.
