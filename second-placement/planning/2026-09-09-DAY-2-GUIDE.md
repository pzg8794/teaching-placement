## Ready-to-use partner interview

**Use the SBTE's existing sheet first.** This is a proposed facilitation script, not a replacement for it. The transcript establishes 60-second partner turns and the interview plan; prompts/timing below are assistant suggestions (onboarding 00:01:00; co-planning 00:57:06–01:00:15).

> Today we're going to practice listening as well as speaking. Partner A has one minute to talk, while Partner B listens without interrupting. Then switch. Choose something you're comfortable sharing. Afterward, check with your partner about what they're happy for you to share with the class.

**Optional prompts if the existing sheet needs extras:**

- What is something you enjoy learning or doing?
- What is a skill you would like to learn this year?
- What helps you when a task is confusing?
- What is a product, app or business you find interesting, and why?

**Simple sequence (planning choice):** 1 minute instructions → 1 minute each partner → 1 minute check what may be shared → brief introductions as time allows → 1-minute debrief.

> What helped you listen? How might listening carefully help someone understand a customer's needs—or help a teammate solve a computer problem?

**Accessibility suggestion:** let students jot keywords, use the posted prompts, or share only one comfortable fact. Ask the SBTE about existing accommodations. Do not require disclosure about family, health, money or identity. No new slides needed: write three prompts on the board if approved.
