# September 23: phone feedback and the seven marketing functions

## Purpose

Students connect the seven marketing functions to a practical phone-based customer experience. The activity uses the *Practical Marketing* page 7 “Send Me SFMOMA” example as the launch, then asks each pair to apply **two** functions to a company or experience of its choice.

## Time

**10 minutes planned; 15 minutes maximum.** Stop on time so the class can return to the remaining notes and reading.

The sequence below is the compressed 10-minute route. The PowerPoint’s displayed timings allow a longer read and discussion, totaling about 12–13 minutes. Use that supported route only within the same 15-minute hard stop, allowing for transitions and the number of pairs.

## Materials

- [Classroom PowerPoint](2026-09-23-Phone-Feedback-Marketing-Functions-Discussion.pptx)
- *Practical Marketing*, page 7
- Students’ marketing-function notes

## Sequence

1. **Read and notice — 1 minute.** Students read the page 7 SFMOMA example and identify how a phone helps customers interact with the museum.
2. **Choose an experience — 1 minute.** Pairs select a company or experience where customers use phones to provide or receive feedback, improve access, or simplify service.
3. **Choose two functions — 1 minute.** Pairs select two of the seven marketing functions; they do not address all seven.
4. **Partners discuss — 3 minutes.** Pairs connect the phone interaction, customer experience or barrier, two functions, and one specific company response.
5. **Share and identify — 4 minutes planned.** Each designated speaker gives a 30-second explanation. Listeners identify the functions used. This allows eight pairs without transition time; budget additional sharing time within the 15-minute maximum for larger classes. If needed, remaining pairs share simultaneously with a neighboring pair so every pair participates without overrunning.
6. **Closing discussion — only if time remains.** Ask which function starts the response and which other function must support it.

## Seven-function reference

1. Marketing-information management — collect and use customer information.
2. Product/service management — develop or improve the offer.
3. Pricing — set what customers pay.
4. Channel management — make the offer available to customers.
5. Promotion — communicate and build awareness.
6. Selling — help customers decide and purchase.
7. Financing — fund the business or help customers pay.

## Partner prompt

> Customers use their phones to tell **[company]** about **[experience, barrier, or need]**. The company uses **[function 1]** and **[function 2]** to **[specific response or improvement]**.

## Participation routine

- Students work in pairs.
- Both partners contribute to the response.
- Students who arrive late become the designated speakers after their partners brief them.
- Each pair receives about 30 seconds to share.
- Listeners identify at least one marketing function and cite the evidence they heard.

## Evidence boundary

The activity summarizes the textbook example without reproducing the licensed page. It does not require students to disclose personal access, finances, or accessibility needs.
