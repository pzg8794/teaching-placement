## Answer quality before the carousel

The worked questions use prior promotion learning and the same forms students encounter in the review: **What is promotion?** followed by a **brief-paragraph explanation** of how Nike's film could promote its brand, with a specific film detail. These are different from the ten Chapter 2 review questions. Students attempt an idea before the explanation is revealed, build the meaning/purpose/example/link, then compare a proficient 3 with possible 4 reasoning.

A separate school-club poster scenario explicitly models how to improve a 3: retain the accurate core, add a useful familiar comparison, explain its relevance and qualify an unsupported result claim. Added reasoning is labelled and revealed on the next click. The school rubric describes proficiency, depth and independence; length, copying or one rehearsed comparison do not guarantee a 4. Students then try another context themselves.

The embedded captioned Nike excerpt is 32 seconds. Use PowerPoint for film playback; PDFs are static companions with a visible poster. Native media packaging, click actions and stream presence were checked; PowerPoint desktop playback was not tested.
