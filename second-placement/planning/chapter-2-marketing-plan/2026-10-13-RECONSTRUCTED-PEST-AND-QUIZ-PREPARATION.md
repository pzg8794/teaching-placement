## Reconstructed PEST classroom activity (approximately 30 minutes)

**Student objective:** Choose one business and target market, then analyze one concrete external factor in each PEST category and distinguish the **factor** from its **possible impacts** on that specific business. PEST covers Political, Economic, Social and Technological external influences (Chapter 2, Figure 2.1, printed p. 20).

| Activity | Target time |
| --- | --- |
| Explain activity, response directions and any subsequently confirmed evaluation rules | 3–5 min |
| Select business and target market | 2 min |
| P, E, S, T: approximately 3 min per category | 12 min |
| Business summary connecting factor and impact | 5 min |
| Remaining buffer / approved Terms to Know work | approximately 6–8 min |

Time amounts are approximate and sum to roughly 28–32 minutes; preserve the lesson's actual clock rather than force an exact duration.

**Required physical worksheet layout:** name, block, date; business name and target customers at top; four clearly headed P/E/S/T sections in the middle; a short business summary at bottom. For **every** P/E/S/T section, students give (1) one business-connected external **factor/example**, (2) one possible **positive (+) impact**, (3) one possible **negative (−) impact**. **“Factor and impact,” not “positive/negative definition.”** No filled-in factor/impact answers are provided to students before they attempt the reasoning.
