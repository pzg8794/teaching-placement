I prepared a short five-minute marketing starter for Thursday. Students first generate their own examples of places where a phone makes a customer experience easier, then share with a partner and connect one example to one marketing function.

I adjusted the activity based on our feedback from today. The students generate the examples before I show the museum model, so I am not giving them the answers in advance. I also limited the starter to one marketing function so we can protect time for the Chapter 1 notes, reading, and vocabulary work.

The route is:

- 45 seconds of quiet individual thinking
- 75 seconds with a partner
- two or three quick examples from the class
- one marketing-function connection and a transition into the lesson

Students can speak, point, write keywords, or use the sentence frame. I attached the PowerPoint for the activity. The museum example remains available as a support if students need one concrete model.
