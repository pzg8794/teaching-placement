## Instructional response

The Thursday and Friday icebreakers use a UofR-style **quick diagnostic / everybody responds** routine:

1. Silent observation or retrieval.
2. A simultaneous response by pointing, writing a letter, or holding up fingers.
3. Immediate answer check with one short explanation.
4. A one-sentence reading connection and direct transition.

This preserves retrieval, choice of response mode, processing time, and visible structure while removing partner-transition and whole-class share-out time.
