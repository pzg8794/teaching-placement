# September 24 Marketing icebreaker — bike pickup access

**Status:** Ready for SBTE review. Public-safe planning artifact.

## Why this opener

The current September 21–25 plan uses a strict 3-minute opener clock and implements the prior pacing feedback: quiet noticing, simultaneous written choices, one concise explanation, and a hard stop before required source work. The September 22 planning update moves vocabulary-quiz and review-question work to Thursday, so this opener is retrieval/connection rather than a new extended activity.

Tomorrow's case is a fictional bicycle-repair pickup problem. The repair is complete, but the shop closes before the student can arrive after school.

## Three-slide routine

1. **Notice (20 seconds):** What detail creates the problem?
2. **Choose (40 seconds + 50-second check):**
   - A — Repair the bike again
   - B — Ask about bike colors
   - C — Change pickup hours
3. **Connect (about 60 seconds):** Reveal **C** and connect **channel management** with **Place**. Ask one question: *How do channel management and Place connect?*

**Hard stop:** 3 minutes. Five minutes is the absolute maximum only if the SBTE deliberately extends the discussion.

## Transition

> We solved access. Now use the academic language in today's quiz, review, and source work.

## Teaching rationale

- Concrete everyday situation before academic terminology.
- No personal disclosure, device, or partner transition required.
- Students can answer by pointing, fingers, writing A/B/C, or speaking.
- One response is enough; do not run a full-room round-robin.
- The scenario reinforces the distinction between the **Place** decision in the 4 Ps and the **channel management** marketing function.
