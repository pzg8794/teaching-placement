# September 10 and 11 ready-to-use plan

This package turns the September 9 planning conversations into usable drafts. The SBTE remains the instructional decision-maker. Confirm the agenda, live Teams location, approved vocabulary file, and file-naming convention before students act.

## Thursday, September 10: workplace norms

### Student outcome

Students create the wording of a shared class contract around four workplace expectations: **on time, prepared, productive, and kind**.

### Display

Use [Our Workplace Norms](2026-09-10-Our-Workplace-Norms-One-Slide.pptx). The slide intentionally leaves the class norms open for students to create.

### 12-minute facilitation script

1. **Frame, 1 minute:** “We are going to run this class with workplace habits. You will help define what those habits look and sound like here.”
2. **Quiet think, 2 minutes:** Each student writes one observable behavior under one of the four expectations. Keywords or a sketch are acceptable.
3. **Partner check, 3 minutes:** Partners compare ideas and improve one statement so anyone could recognize the behavior.
4. **Class build, 5 minutes:** Collect one or two ideas under each heading. Ask, “Can we observe this?” and “Would this help someone learn or work?”
5. **Close, 1 minute:** Read the draft aloud. Tell students the SBTE will confirm the final class contract.

### Guardrails

- The four headings stay fixed; students create the supporting behaviors.
- Use specific, observable wording. Example: “Open the assigned file when class begins.”
- Do not promise that every suggestion becomes a rule.
- Students may contribute in writing, through a partner, or aloud.

## Friday, September 11: Chapter 1 and Microsoft 365

### Display

Use [Chapter 1 Brain Teasers and Microsoft 365 Workflow](2026-09-11-Chapter-1-Brain-Teasers-and-M365-Workflow.pptx).

### Suggested 45-minute sequence

| Time | Teacher move | Student evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Run the purchase brain teaser while attendance happens | One marketing influence with a reason |
| 5–10 | Connect two answers to product, price, place, or promotion | Students explain the connection |
| 10–18 | Model the class folder and Archive structure | Students can open the class folder |
| 18–23 | Confirm the approved naming pattern | Students rename the correct file |
| 23–32 | Guide the approved vocabulary-file download | The file opens from the class folder |
| 32–40 | Model the Teams attachment path | The correct attachment appears |
| 40–45 | Run the exit check | Each student shows one readiness indicator |

### Exact opening language

“Today we will connect a Chapter 1 marketing question to a workplace skill: keeping digital work organized. You may use a fictional purchase. After the discussion, follow one step at a time on your laptop. Stop at each checkpoint so we can solve problems together.”

### If technology fails

Keep the brain teaser and Chapter 1 discussion. Model the folder and file-name structure on the projector without requiring students to complete it. Record the exact access problem for the SBTE or district support. Do not invent a new sign-in method or post a replacement assignment.

## Brain teaser bank for Friday and next week

The Friday deck includes five additional openings grounded in Chapter 1:

1. Goods, services, and ideas
2. Limited resources
3. Marketing concept
4. Marketing mix
5. Target market

Use one per day. Give students 60 seconds of quiet thinking before discussion. Accept a written response, partner report, or fictional example. Ask for a reason rather than a single guessed word.
