## ♿ UDL and Inclusion Supports

### 👀 Multiple Representation
- 👀 Project the exact menu path and keep it visible during startup.
- 👀 Use one shared visual for command vocabulary such as move, turn, loop, and agent.
- 👀 Show the objective and the success condition before students start coding.

### ✍️ Multiple Action / Expression
- ✍️ Allow students to plan with arrows or pseudocode before placing blocks.
- ✍️ Use partners strategically so one student navigates while the other checks directions.
- ✍️ Provide a “help checkpoint” routine: read objective, test one block, ask after trying one fix.

### ❤️ Multiple Engagement
- ❤️ Use challenge progression and visible level goals to sustain attention.
- ❤️ Normalize being stuck as part of coding, especially in the boss-level and timed tasks.
- ❤️ Keep free-build desires acknowledged but separated from the current lesson purpose.

## 🗣️ Teacher Moves / Script / Routines

- 🗣️ Start by naming the lesson purpose and separating it from free build mode.
- 🗣️ Model the exact clicks to reach the lesson world, then pause for students to mirror.
- 🗣️ Require students to read the challenge objective before placing blocks.
- 🗣️ Stop mid-lesson to share one debugging pattern that is working.
- 🗣️ Close with a brief reflection on the challenge, bug, and fix.

## 🐞 Common Errors and Debugging

- 🐞 Students may get lost in sign-in or library navigation before reaching the lesson.
- 🐞 Students may ignore written directions and brute-force the level.
- 🐞 Timed or dual-goal levels can overload students if they do not break the task into parts.
- 🐞 Some students may confuse avatar movement and agent movement.

## ↔️ Extensions and Simplifications

- ↔️ Extension: ask students to optimize a working solution with fewer blocks or more elegant loop use.
- ↔️ Simplification: provide a partially completed block stack or a paper path plan first.
- ↔️ Extension: add a reflection comparing two different solutions to the same level.
- ↔️ Simplification: assign a fixed partner role split for navigation, coding, and checking.
