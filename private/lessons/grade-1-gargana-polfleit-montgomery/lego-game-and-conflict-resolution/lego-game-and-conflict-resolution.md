## ♿ UDL and Inclusion Supports

### 👀 Multiple Representation
- 👀 Keep rules visible with icons and one-sentence language.
- 👀 Model one repair phrase and one help phrase.
- 👀 Use floor/table examples to show what “ready to build” looks like.

### ✍️ Multiple Action / Expression
- ✍️ Allow students to point to a rule card instead of speaking immediately.
- ✍️ Provide assigned jobs so each student has a clear role.
- ✍️ Use quick reset spaces or pause cards for students who become dysregulated.

### ❤️ Multiple Engagement
- ❤️ Make the goal concrete and shared so students see why cooperation matters.
- ❤️ Use frequent praise for repair, not only for the final product.
- ❤️ Keep the task hands-on and short-cycle.

## 🔧 How to Improve Inclusion Next Time

- 🔧 Add explicit visual sentence stems for conflict repair.
- 🔧 Record which roles consistently trigger conflict and redesign those role expectations.
- 🔧 Prepare a calmer “restart routine” so students can rejoin without shame.
- 🔧 Add a teacher observation checklist to `artifacts/` for documenting collaboration growth.

## 🛠️ Materials and Setup

- 🛠️ LEGO or maker materials
- 🛠️ Game/build prompt
- 🛠️ Visible rule cards
- 🛠️ Teacher observation sheet

## 🗣️ Teacher Moves / Script / Routines

- 🗣️ Preview one rule, one role, and one repair phrase before materials go out.
- 🗣️ Interrupt quickly when conflict threatens access to the task.
- 🗣️ Narrate successful repair as evidence of learning, not just behavior compliance.
- 🗣️ Close with a short reflection on how the group kept the activity going.

## 🐞 Common Errors and Debugging

- 🐞 Students may focus on winning or building faster than working together.
- 🐞 Conflict can erase the instructional goal if there is no visible restart routine.
- 🐞 Some students may need explicit language frames to re-enter the activity.

## ↔️ Extensions and Simplifications

- ↔️ Extension: have students help generate the next version of the class build rules.
- ↔️ Simplification: reduce the number of shared parts and shorten the task cycle.
- ↔️ Extension: compare what makes a build/game rule effective versus confusing.
