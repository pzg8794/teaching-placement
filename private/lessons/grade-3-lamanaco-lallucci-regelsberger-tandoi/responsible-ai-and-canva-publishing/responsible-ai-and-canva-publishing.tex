\section*{UDL and Inclusion Supports}
\subsection*{Multiple Representation}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Model one finished example and one in-progress revision.
\item Use a checklist for message, image, text, and source use.
\item Provide oral and visual explanation of AI expectations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Multiple Action / Expression}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Allow students to design with text, image, voice, or paired editing.
\item Use templates to reduce blank-page overwhelm.
\item Provide sentence stems for attribution and audience explanation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Multiple Engagement}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Anchor the work in a real publishing purpose.
\item Let students choose among a few formats within a controlled template set.
\item Treat revision as product improvement rather than correction.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Teacher Moves / Script / Routines}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Open by naming what the artifact is for and who it is for.
\item Model one design decision that improves clarity.
\item Pause to discuss what counts as acceptable AI support versus misuse.
\item Close with a short reflection on how the design communicates to an audience.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Common Errors and Debugging}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Students may over-focus on decoration and under-explain the message.
\item AI-generated text or images may be copied without reflection unless attribution is modeled.
\item Open-ended design space can overload students without templates.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Extensions and Simplifications}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Extension: compare two published products and discuss which is more responsible or audience-aware.
\item Simplification: reduce choices to one template and one message goal.
\item Extension: add a revision note describing what changed and why.
\end{itemize}
