## 🎯 Main Focus
Hands-on electrical circuit building with batteries, switches, conductors, and bulbs.

## 🎯 Learning Targets
- Students identify the basic parts of a simple circuit.
- Students assemble a working closed circuit that lights a bulb.
- Students explain why polarity, connections, and full contact matter.

## 🪜 Lesson Flow
- Model the materials and the correct positive/negative connections.
- Teams build and test a circuit, then revise if the bulb does not light.
- Students explain what made their circuit work during a short share-out.
