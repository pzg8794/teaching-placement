## ♿ UDL and Inclusion Supports

### 👀 Multiple Representation
- 👀 Introduce AI with a video, teacher talk, and concrete examples from daily life.
- 👀 Use visible category labels and color coding when sorting examples.
- 👀 Keep the difference between “example” and “guess” explicit.

### ✍️ Multiple Action / Expression
- ✍️ Allow students to answer by sorting, pointing, talking, or dragging items.
- ✍️ Pair students so one can navigate while the other explains.
- ✍️ Provide printed category cards for off-screen rehearsal before the digital task.

### ❤️ Multiple Engagement
- ❤️ Connect ocean cleanup to a meaningful real-world problem.
- ❤️ Alternate short discussion bursts with active sorting.
- ❤️ Treat mistakes as useful training data, not failure.

## 🔧 How to Improve Inclusion Next Time

- 🔧 Add picture-supported category cards for students who need more off-screen scaffolding.
- 🔧 Include a bias/error conversation using one intentionally tricky example.
- 🔧 Prepare sentence stems such as “I put it here because…” and “The computer guessed wrong because…”
- 🔧 Save screenshots of correct and incorrect classifications in `artifacts/` as evidence examples.

## 🗣️ Teacher Moves / Script / Routines

- 🗣️ Model login and launch before discussing AI concepts.
- 🗣️ Stop after the first classification to ask what the computer is learning.
- 🗣️ Normalize wrong guesses as part of training the model.
- 🗣️ Close with a short reflection on where students see sorting or prediction in real life.

## 🐞 Common Errors and Debugging

- 🐞 Students may confuse AI with a human-like robot instead of a trained model.
- 🐞 Login friction can consume too much time if not tightly modeled.
- 🐞 Students may sort by appearance without explaining their category logic.

## ↔️ Extensions and Simplifications

- ↔️ Extension: compare two hard-to-sort examples and debate the best category.
- ↔️ Simplification: reduce the task to fewer categories and more teacher-led sorting.
- ↔️ Extension: connect the lesson to responsible AI and why bad data causes bad results.
