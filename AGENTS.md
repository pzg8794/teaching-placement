## Teaching presentation and visual-material standard

For any teaching-placement presentation, slide deck, PDF, flyer, poster, handout,
or other visual teaching material, read and follow
`docs/TEACHING-PRESENTATION-AND-VISUAL-MATERIAL-STANDARD.md` before creating or
revising the artifact.

Core defaults:
- ground visuals in the newest placement/SBTE/source state;
- reuse de-identified student examples and language when appropriate;
- every visual must teach, distinguish, cue, or support a student action;
- use distinct visual evidence for distinct concepts;
- use progressive reveal when students should supply the answer before the term appears;
- keep the style age-appropriate, mature, readable, and consistent;
- when a classroom deck is requested, the final deliverables are editable PowerPoint
  plus a checked PDF unless the user explicitly requests another format;
- render and visually inspect the full deck and exported PDF before release;
- preserve the repository privacy boundary at all times.
