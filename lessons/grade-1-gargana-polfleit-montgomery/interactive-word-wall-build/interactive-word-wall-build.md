## ♿ UDL and Inclusion Supports

### 👀 Multiple Representation
- 👀 Pair every word with a picture, color cue, and concrete object.
- 👀 Use teacher-modeled examples before students create their own pieces.
- 👀 Keep instructions anchored to one visible exemplar at a time.

### ✍️ Multiple Action / Expression
- ✍️ Allow drawing, speaking, pointing, or building as valid ways to show understanding.
- ✍️ Use partner roles: builder, holder, explainer, scanner.
- ✍️ Offer pre-cut or partially prepared materials for students who fatigue quickly.

### ❤️ Multiple Engagement
- ❤️ Let students choose which word or concept they represent.
- ❤️ Use scanning, recording, or showing as motivating interactive elements.
- ❤️ Celebrate each added piece so the wall becomes a visible cumulative success marker.

- 🔧 Add a one-page visual build checklist at each table.

- 🔧 Offer a no-QR fallback path where students can point to a recorded explanation instead of scanning independently.

## 🛠️ Materials and Setup

- 🛠️ Word cards
- 🛠️ QR links or short recorded videos
- 🛠️ Maker materials / LEGO or build pieces
- 🛠️ Display space
- 🛠️ Student or teacher scanning device

## 🗣️ Teacher Moves / Script / Routines

- 🗣️ Start with one finished example and reverse-engineer how it was made.
- 🗣️ Assign one clear role before materials are distributed.
- 🗣️ Pause the room to compare two strong representation choices.
- 🗣️ Close by having students explain how the wall helps another learner understand the word.

## 🐞 Common Errors and Debugging

- 🐞 Students may focus on decoration instead of meaning.
- 🐞 QR or video links may fail without a backup display plan.
- 🐞 Shared materials can create conflict if roles are not explicit.

## ↔️ Extensions and Simplifications

- ↔️ Extension: add a short student-recorded explanation to each entry.
- ↔️ Simplification: limit each team to one word, one image, and one explanation.
- ↔️ Extension: sort wall entries into categories and ask students to explain the pattern.
