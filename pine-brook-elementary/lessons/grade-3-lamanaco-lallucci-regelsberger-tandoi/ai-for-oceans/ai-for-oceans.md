If AI seems too complex for your class, consider focusing on classification and sorting of items. Focus on concrete experiences (e.g., sorting pictures of fish vs. trash) rather than abstract AI theory. | Emphasize cause and effect ("If we show the computer many pictures of fish, it learns to find fish.")Use a short, illustrated social story about a friendly robot learning to tell fish apart from trash to help the ocean. Alternatively, show a simple, captioned video explaining how computers learn by seeing many examples and making guesses.
