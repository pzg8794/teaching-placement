# Lesson 2 AI Directions

## Student-Facing Directions for Using AI in Lesson 2

Today you are using AI as a build partner. You already have a topic and a project guide, or you will recover it quickly with teacher help. Then you will use the `AI Assistant` to help you build your project one part at a time.

## Your Job

1. Reopen or recover your topic and guide from Lesson 1.
2. Check that your topic still fits computer science.
3. Open your project tool and start with Part 1 of your guide.
4. Use the `AI Assistant` when you need help to:
   - turn one guide part into notes or bullets
   - explain something more clearly
   - make the language easier to understand
   - check whether your section fits your topic and guide
5. Build Part 2 and Part 3 the same way.
6. Stop at the midpoint checkpoint and compare your project to your guide.
7. Record what you finished today and what still needs work for Lesson 3.

## How to Use Each Tool

### Use the `AI Assistant`

Use this tool to help you build from your guide, one section at a time.

Prompt ideas:
- `My topic is ____. Part 1 of my guide is ____. Give me 3 to 5 notes or bullet points I can use on one slide.`
- `My topic is ____. Here is my draft section: ____. Does it match my guide and my computer science goal?`
- `My topic is ____. Help me explain this part in clear Grade 4 language: ____.`
- `My topic is ____. What details are missing from this part of my project: ____?`

### Use Your Project Tool

Use your slideshow, poster, booklet, or simple site to build the real project.

Build one part at a time:
- Part 1
- Part 2
- Part 3
- optional Part 4 or 5 if your guide includes them

## Important Rule

Do not paste the AI answer into your whole project without checking it. Use the AI to help you build each section, then decide what to keep, change, or leave out. You should be able to explain what you built and why it fits your guide.

## Help Routine

If you get stuck:
1. Look at your guide.
2. Finish one small step.
3. Ask your partner to read the step with you.
4. Use one AI prompt.
5. Ask the teacher one clear question.
