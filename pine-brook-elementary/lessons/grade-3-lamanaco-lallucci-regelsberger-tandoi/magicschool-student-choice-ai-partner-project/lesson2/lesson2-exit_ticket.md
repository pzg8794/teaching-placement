# Lesson 2 Exit Ticket

Use this as a quick closure check for Session 2: Build Step by Step.

## Google Form Title

`MagicSchool Project Exit Ticket - Lesson 2 Build Step by Step`

## Google Form Description

Today we used AI as a build partner. This exit ticket shows what you built, how AI helped you, and what you still need to do next. Use your own words. Do not include private information.

## Exit Ticket Questions

1. What is your name?
2. What class are you in?
   - Baris
   - Fowler
   - Schrank
   - Autore
   - Other
3. What topic are you working on?
4. What kind of project are you building?
   - Slideshow
   - Poster or one-pager
   - Booklet
   - Simple site
   - Other teacher-approved product
5. Which part or sections did you build today?
6. How did the `AI Assistant` help you today?
   - It helped me recover or improve my guide
   - It helped me turn one guide part into notes or bullets
   - It helped me make my writing clearer
   - It helped me check if a section fit my topic
   - I still needed a lot of help
   - It did not help me very much
7. What is one thing you kept from the AI?
8. What is one thing you changed, fixed, or decided on your own?
9. How well does your project match your guide right now?
   - 1 = Not yet
   - 2 = A little
   - 3 = Some parts match
   - 4 = Most parts match
   - 5 = It matches well
10. Did you follow the AI safety rules today?
    - Yes
    - Mostly
    - I need help remembering them
11. What still needs work before Lesson 3?
12. What is your next step?

## Suggested Teacher Settings

- Make questions 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 11, and 12 required.
- Collect email addresses only if your school normally uses that setup.
- Keep question order fixed.
- Limit to one response if students are signed in.
- Suggested confirmation message:
  - `Thanks. Your progress is saved. Be ready to revise and finish in Lesson 3.`
