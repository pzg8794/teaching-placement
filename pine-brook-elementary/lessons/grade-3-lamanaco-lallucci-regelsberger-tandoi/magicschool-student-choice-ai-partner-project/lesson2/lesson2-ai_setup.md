# Lesson 2 AI Setup

Use this setup for Session 2: Build Step by Step.

## Goal

Students leave Lesson 2 with:
- their topic recovered or confirmed
- a usable project guide ready to follow
- at least 2 to 3 built sections or slides
- evidence that they used AI to clarify, not to take over the work

## Teacher Setup Steps

1. Open the teacher-managed MagicSchool or MagicStudent space for the class.
2. Make sure students enter using the school-approved access method, such as SSO or a teacher room code.
3. Limit students to the tools needed for Lesson 2 only:
   - `AI Assistant`
4. Keep the student project tool ready in a second tab:
   - `Google Slides` is the default Grade 4 build tool
   - allow poster, booklet, or simple site only if already approved in Lesson 1
5. Project the approved prompt frames before students begin.
6. Review the AI safety rules again:
   - no student names or private information
   - AI is a helper, not the final answer
   - students must check whether the AI response fits the guide and the CS goal
   - teacher can monitor activity
7. Begin with a short recovery checkpoint so students can confirm:
   - topic
   - CS connection
   - product type
   - guide with 3 to 5 parts, sections, or questions
8. Tell students that today they are building their project one part at a time, not decorating everything at once.

## Tool Roles

### `AI Assistant`

Use this tool to:
- recover or rebuild a simple guide if a student did not finish Lesson 1
- turn one guide part into notes, bullets, or a small draft section
- simplify language for Grade 4 readability
- check whether a draft section still matches the student topic and CS goal
- point out missing details or unclear explanations

### `Google Slides` or Other Approved Product Tool

Use this tool to:
- build the actual project artifact
- create one section at a time from the approved guide
- show progress toward the final Lesson 3 product

## Lesson 2 Workflow

1. Student reopens or recovers the topic and guide from Lesson 1.
2. Student confirms the topic is still connected to computer science.
3. Student opens the project tool and builds Part 1.
4. Student uses the `AI Assistant` only when needed to:
   - clarify a part
   - add missing details
   - simplify wording
   - check whether the section fits the plan
5. Student builds Part 2 and Part 3 in the same way.
6. Student pauses for a midpoint check against the guide.
7. Student records what was finished and what still needs to be done in Lesson 3.

## Teacher Look-Fors

- students begin with the guide, not random decorating
- students work in the assigned tool flow
- students build one section at a time
- students can explain how each section connects to the topic and CS goal
- students use AI to clarify or improve, not to replace their own thinking
- students leave with visible project progress and a clear next step
