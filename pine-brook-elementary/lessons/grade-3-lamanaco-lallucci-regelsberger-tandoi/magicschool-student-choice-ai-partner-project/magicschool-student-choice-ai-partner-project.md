# 🔵 MagicSchool Student-Choice AI Partner Project

**Color Code:** 🟢 Lessons

> **📌 What This Is:** A 3-session lesson packet for helping students use MagicSchool as a safe AI partner while they create a project on a topic they choose.
> **🧭 Start Here When:** You want one reusable project sequence that teaches brainstorming, planning, building, revising, and responsible AI use together.
> **🎯 Main Goal:** Help students use AI as a partner for thinking, not a replacement for their ideas, voice, or decision-making.

## 🎯 School Planning Goals + Inclusion

- **What is expected:** Students use a supervised AI tool to brainstorm, plan, build, and revise a project while keeping ownership of ideas and decisions.
- **What we achieved:** This packet combines project-based learning, digital literacy, and responsible AI routines in one sequence.
- **What needs improvement:** Add direct classroom evidence after teaching the lesson and save at least one student artifact.
- **How to improve:** Tighten timing after first use, add sample prompts that reflect actual student needs, and document which scaffolds are most effective.
- **Inclusion focus:** Use chunked directions, visual prompt frames, teacher think-alouds, flexible product choices, partner roles, and reduced-text options.

- **This lesson should especially support:** Students who benefit from a visible process for choosing a topic, checking AI suggestions, and revising toward a goal.

## 🧠 Known Supports For This Class

- Visible routines help students stay focused on the goal instead of getting lost in the tool.
- Partner talk can reduce language load before students type.
- Teacher conferencing is especially useful when students need help deciding what to keep, change, or reject from AI output.

## 🪜 Scaffolds To Use In This Lesson

- Keep the `Ask, Check, Choose, Change, Credit/Explain` routine visible all three sessions.
- Offer prompt starters, sentence frames, and product exemplars.
- Let students draw, dictate, or orally rehearse before typing.
- Use a teacher-led or partner-supported version for K-2 or higher-support learners.

## ⚠️ Likely Friction Points

- Students may copy AI language without checking whether it matches their topic or goal.
- Some students may pick a topic that is too broad and need help narrowing it.
- Students may think the AI answer is automatically correct unless the checking routine stays visible.

## 🤝 Independence / Partnering / Teacher Support Plan

- Teacher models responsible prompting and thinking aloud before release.
- Partners can take turns as `prompt reader`, `idea checker`, or `builder`.
- Teacher circulates for fast conferences around topic fit, accuracy, and revision choices.

## 🛠️ Materials and Teaching Moves

- Teacher device and projector
- Student devices with teacher-managed MagicSchool access
- Planning sheet or digital planning doc
- Product creation tools such as Google Slides, Docs, Sites, or drawing tools
- Printed or projected rubric and prompt frames

## ✅ Assessment and Proof

- Student chooses a topic and creates a plan that still sounds like their own idea.
- Student uses AI to support the work, not replace the work.
- Student checks AI output for fit, accuracy, and clarity before using it.
- Student completes a project artifact and explains how AI helped.
- Teacher collects one proof item such as a planning sheet, screenshot, conference note, reflection, or final product.
