# Lesson 1 Checkpoint Rubric

Use this rubric to assess Session 1: Brainstorm and Plan.

| Category | Beginning | Developing | Meeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Topic Ownership and Idea Clarity | K-2: Needs help choosing a topic or explaining the idea. 3-5: Topic is too broad, unclear, or mostly chosen by others. | K-2: Chooses a topic with support and can say a little about it. 3-5: Chooses a topic and gives a basic reason, but the focus may still be broad. | K-2: Chooses a clear topic and explains why it matters. 3-5: Chooses a focused topic, explains the goal clearly, and keeps ownership of the idea. |
| CS Connection | K-2: Needs help connecting the topic to technology or computer science. 3-5: Cannot yet explain how the topic connects to computer science. | K-2: Can name a simple technology connection with support. 3-5: Gives a partial CS connection, but it may still be vague or weak. | K-2: Can explain a simple connection between the topic and technology or computing. 3-5: Clearly explains how the topic connects to computer science, technology, AI, digital safety, or problem-solving. |
| Planning with AI | K-2: Uses AI only with heavy support and does not yet connect the response to a guide or plan. 3-5: Prompts are incomplete, or the guide mostly repeats AI output without student decisions. | K-2: Uses AI with support to make a simple guide. 3-5: Uses AI to help generate a guide and makes some choices, but the structure still needs teacher help. | K-2: Uses AI support to help make a simple guide the student understands. 3-5: Uses AI to brainstorm and organize a high-level guide with 3 to 5 parts, sections, or questions while keeping student choice visible. |
| Responsible and Safe AI Use | K-2: Needs repeated reminders not to share private information and not to copy everything from AI. 3-5: Uses AI unsafely or accepts responses without checking. | K-2: Follows safety rules with reminders. 3-5: Usually follows safety rules and checks some responses, but may still overtrust AI. | K-2: Uses AI safely with teacher support and can say one rule. 3-5: Uses AI safely, checks responses for fit and accuracy, and explains what the student decided independently. |
| Readiness for Lesson 2 | K-2: Does not yet have a clear topic or guide to follow. 3-5: Topic or guide is incomplete and not yet ready to use. | K-2: Has a topic and partial guide with support. 3-5: Has a topic and partial guide, but some parts still need clarification. | K-2: Has a clear topic and simple guide ready for the next lesson. 3-5: Has an approved topic, a clear CS connection, and a high-level guide with 3 to 5 parts, sections, or questions ready for implementation in Lesson 2. |
