# Lesson 1 AI Directions

## Student-Facing Directions for Using AI in Lesson 1

Today you are using AI as a brainstorming and planning partner. You will use `Idea Generator` first to come up with possible project topics. Then you will use the `AI Assistant` to check, improve, and organize your topic into a project guide.

## Your Job

1. Think of an interest or topic you care about.
2. Open `Idea Generator` and ask for project ideas connected to computer science.
3. Read the ideas carefully and choose one or two that match your goal.
4. Pick one topic that is interesting, school-appropriate, and small enough for a short project.
5. Open the `AI Assistant` and use it to:
   - check whether your topic is really connected to computer science
   - improve or narrow your topic if it is too broad
   - create a high-level guide with 3 to 5 parts, sections, or questions
6. Review the guide and decide whether it fits your topic and project goal.
7. Record your final topic, your project type, and the guide you plan to follow.

## How to Use Each Tool

### Use `Idea Generator`

Use this tool to come up with possible project topics.

Prompt ideas:
- `My interest is ____. Give me 4 computer-science project ideas for grades 4-5.`
- `Help me turn my interest in ____ into a computer-science project topic.`
- `Give me 4 project topic ideas that connect ____, technology, and computer science.`

### Use the `AI Assistant`

Use this tool to test, narrow, and organize one topic into a usable guide.

Prompt ideas:
- `My topic is ____. Is this really connected to computer science? Explain how.`
- `My topic is ____. Help me make it smaller and clearer for a short project.`
- `My topic is ____. Give me a high-level guide with 3 to 5 parts, sections, or questions I could use for my project.`
- `I want my project to be a slideshow, poster, booklet, or simple site. Which topic choice fits best for that kind of project?`

## Important Rule

Do not copy the AI answer into your project. Use the AI to help you think, choose, and plan. You should be able to explain why you picked your topic and why you agreed with the guide.
