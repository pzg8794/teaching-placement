# Lesson 1 Exit Ticket

Use this as a quick closure check for Session 1: Brainstorm and Plan.

## Google Form Title

`MagicSchool Project Exit Ticket - Lesson 1 Brainstorm and Plan`

## Google Form Description

Today we used AI as a thinking partner. This exit ticket shows what you chose, how you planned, and how AI helped you. Use your own words. Do not include private information.

## Exit Ticket Questions

1. What is your name?
2. What class are you in?
   - Lamanaco
   - Lallucci
   - Regelsberger
   - Tandoi
   - Other
3. What topic did you choose for your project?
4. Why did you choose this topic?
5. What kind of project do you want to make?
   - Slideshow
   - Poster or one-pager
   - Booklet
   - Simple site
   - I am not sure yet
6. What are 3 parts, sections, or questions in your project guide?
7. How did MagicSchool help you today?
   - It helped me think of topic ideas
   - It helped me narrow my topic
   - It helped me check the CS connection
   - It helped me organize my guide
   - I still needed a lot of help
   - It did not help me very much
8. What is one AI idea or suggestion you kept?
9. What is one AI idea or suggestion you changed or did not use? Why?
10. How sure are you about your project guide right now?
    - 1 = I am very unsure
    - 2 = I need more help
    - 3 = I am getting there
    - 4 = I feel ready
    - 5 = I am very ready
11. Did you follow the AI safety rules today?
    - Yes
    - Mostly
    - I need help remembering them
12. Which safety rule is most important when using AI at school?
    - Do not share private information
    - AI can help, but it should not do all the thinking
    - Check if the AI answer fits your goal
    - Be respectful and school-appropriate
    - All of these
13. What is your next step for Lesson 2?

## Suggested Teacher Settings

- Make questions 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, and 13 required.
- Collect email addresses only if your school normally uses that setup.
- Keep question order fixed.
- Limit to one response if students are signed in.
- Suggested confirmation message:
  - `Thanks. Your plan is saved. Be ready to use it in Lesson 2.`
