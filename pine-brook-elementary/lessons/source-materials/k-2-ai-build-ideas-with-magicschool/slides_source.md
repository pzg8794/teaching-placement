# K-2 AI Build Ideas with MagicSchool

Student-facing goal: Students use MagicSchool as an idea helper to brainstorm what they can build with the material at their table, then choose one idea, build it, and share how AI helped.

## Slide 1: AI Build Ideas
Ask. Choose. Build.

## Slide 2: Today We Will
Ask AI for ideas.
Choose one idea.
Build with our table material.
Share what we made.

Grade 2 bridge: 2-3.IC.1, 2-3.CY.1, 2-3.DL.3, and 2-3.NSD.3.

## Slide 14: Teacher Notes - Inclusion Supports
Use visual directions and repeated sentence frames.
Allow students to copy prompts, say prompts aloud, or work with a partner.
Give each table a material choice and a clear role: typer, reader, builder, sharer.
Accept different demonstrations: physical build, oral explanation, pointing, partner share, or photo.
Keep AI prompts short and predictable.
