## ⚡ Quick Scan

- **Class block folder:** `grade-4-baris-fowler-schrank-autore`
- **Audience type:** Intermediate learners who can handle complex digital tasks when setup friction, debugging, and persistence are explicitly coached.
- **Design lens:** Use challenge-and-debug slides with clear click paths, bold timers/checkpoints, and visible help routines.
- **Use this for:** pacing, partnering, slide design, tool setup, and support planning

## 👥 Recurring Support Needs

- Clear setup routines for sign-in, menu navigation, and device troubleshooting before concept work starts.
- Visible success criteria so students can separate “I am stuck” from “I still have a next step.”
- Support for productive struggle: students benefit from hints, not rescues, when challenge levels rise.
- Opportunities to verbalize strategy and compare debugging choices with peers.

## ✅ What Helps This Class

- Use highly visible checkpoints and bold stop-here slides before students disappear into the task.
- Show exactly where to click and what to read when tools have multiple menus or confusing screens.
- Keep the language of persistence positive, specific, and tied to the lesson goal.

## ⚠️ What To Adjust Next

- Add a stronger what-to-do-if-setup-breaks branch to every digital lesson.
- Capture more examples of student debugging talk so future scaffolds match real friction.

## 🎨 Presentation Rules For This Class

- Keep slides low-density and action-forward.
- Use repeated icons and colors so students can re-find the same kinds of information quickly.
- Make the help routine visible before students need it.
- Show what success looks like, not only what to do first.
