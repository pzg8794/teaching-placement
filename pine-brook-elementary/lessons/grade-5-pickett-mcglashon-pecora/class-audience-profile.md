## ⚡ Quick Scan

- **Class block folder:** `grade-5-pickett-mcglashon-pecora`
- **Audience type:** Older elementary learners who can manage autonomy and longer projects when expectations, accountability, and revision structures stay visible.
- **Design lens:** Use sleek challenge decks with strong checkpointing, planning prompts, and visible revision expectations.
- **Use this for:** pacing, partnering, slide design, tool setup, and support planning

## 👥 Recurring Support Needs

- Planning support for multi-step work so students do not rush to the tool without a clear strategy.
- Visible accountability for partner contributions, especially in robotics, design, and project lessons.
- Support with conditional reasoning, testing, and iteration when tasks become more open-ended.
- Clear reminders to document or explain thinking, not just finish the build or code.

## ✅ What Helps This Class

- Use checklists that feel age-respectful but still make expectations explicit.
- Give students comparison examples so they can self-assess without waiting for the teacher every time.
- Build in revision pauses that ask students to justify a design or coding choice.

## ⚠️ What To Adjust Next

- Strengthen the final reflection and proof-of-learning moves in project lessons.
- Use clearer role language when advanced students start moving too quickly past their partner.

## 🎨 Presentation Rules For This Class

- Keep slides low-density and action-forward.
- Use repeated icons and colors so students can re-find the same kinds of information quickly.
- Make the help routine visible before students need it.
- Show what success looks like, not only what to do first.
