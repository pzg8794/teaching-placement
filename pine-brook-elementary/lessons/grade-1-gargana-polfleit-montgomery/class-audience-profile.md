## ⚡ Quick Scan

- **Class block folder:** `grade-1-gargana-polfleit-montgomery`
- **Audience type:** Primary learners who can take on playful structure when directions stay concrete, visible, and socially supported.
- **Design lens:** Use playful visuals, concrete verbs, and a very visible my turn / our turn / your turn rhythm.
- **Use this for:** pacing, partnering, slide design, tool setup, and support planning

## 👥 Recurring Support Needs

- Clear partner roles, turn-taking cues, and conflict-repair language during shared materials work.
- Movement, pointing, and physical modeling to connect ideas to actions.
- Sentence frames and visual reminders when students need help explaining their thinking.
- Fast resets when attention drifts or waiting time gets too long.

## ✅ What Helps This Class

- Use color-coded steps and repeat the same icons for build, try, and share moments.
- Keep examples tangible and linked to the exact materials in students hands.
- Plan for partner talk before whole-group explanation so more students can rehearse.

## ⚠️ What To Adjust Next

- Make the help routine more visible so students ask for support without stalling the room.
- Trim multi-step verbal directions into short call-and-response chunks.

## 🎨 Presentation Rules For This Class

- Keep slides low-density and action-forward.
- Use repeated icons and colors so students can re-find the same kinds of information quickly.
- Make the help routine visible before students need it.
- Show what success looks like, not only what to do first.
