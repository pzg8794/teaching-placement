---

## 🧭 Program Flow

1. **100-Hour Field Experience**
2. **Student Teaching 1**
3. **Student Teaching 2**

This graphic frames the placement as a progression from observation and gradual release into fuller instructional responsibility.

---

## 🗓️ Field Experience Expectations

- At least **3 days per week**
- Runs from **opening day through the end of October**
- Includes:
  - field observation
  - tutoring
  - assistant teaching
  - co-instruction
- Includes **1 field visit** by university supervisor / faculty advisor
- Includes **mid-placement self and formative evaluation**

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Student Teaching 1 Expectations

- **20 instructional days** or **4 weeks**
- Happens immediately after field experience
- In the **same placement school**
- Includes:
  - co-teaching or individual instructional responsibility
  - implementation of innovative lessons
  - **1 to 2 field visits**
  - summative evaluation at completion

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Student Teaching 2 Expectations

- **1 week or more** of pre-instructional acclimation
- **40 instructional days** or **8 weeks**
- In a **second placement school**
- Grade-band expectation:
  - if first placement was grades `10–12`, second should be `7–9`
  - or the reverse
- Includes:
  - co-teaching or individual planning/instructional responsibility
  - at least **3 daily class sections**
  - at least **2 preparations**
  - innovative unit design
  - edTPA artifacts
  - **3 field visits**
  - midpoint and summative evaluation

---

## 🎯 How To Use This For Lesson Improvement

- Use it as a **requirements check**:
  - Are lessons showing instructional responsibility clearly?
  - Are the innovative lessons actually documented well enough?
  - Is there evidence of planning, implementation, and reflection?
- Use it as a **reflection frame**:
  - what the placement expects
  - what has already been taught
  - what still needs stronger evidence or documentation
