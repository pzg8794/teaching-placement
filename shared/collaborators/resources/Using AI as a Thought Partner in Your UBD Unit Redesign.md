### ***Sample Prompts for Stages 1–3 \+ Iterative Design***

Below are examples of how you can use our course AI agent as a collaborative partner while redesigning your unit using the **Understanding by Design (UbD)** framework. Use these prompts to brainstorm, refine ideas, check alignment, and push your thinking about meaningful technology integration.

# **Stage 1: Desired Results**

### ***Learning Goals • Standards • Transfer • Essential Questions***

**1\. Unpack the standards**  
 *“Here are the standards for my unit. Help me turn them into clear, student-friendly learning goals and propose 2–3 essential questions that drive inquiry. Highlight places where technology might support deeper understanding.”*

**2\. Diagnose my Stage 1 draft**  
 *“Here is my initial list of goals, skills, and understandings. Where are the gaps or redundancies? Which goals are too broad or too narrow? Suggest revisions using UbD principles.”*

**3\. Generate transfer goals**  
 *“Given this content, what might long-term transfer goals look like? Give me 3–4 options, including at least one that meaningfully incorporates digital tools or computational thinking.”*

# **Stage 2: Evidence & Assessment**

### ***Performance Tasks • Products • Tech-Enhanced Evidence of Learning***

**4\. Critique my assessment ideas**  
 *“Here are the assessments I’m considering. Which align most strongly with my learning goals? Suggest tech-enabled alternatives that could provide richer evidence of understanding.”*

**5\. Design an authentic tech-integrated task**  
 *“Help me design an authentic performance task that uses technology in meaningful ways—not just efficiency. Show me how the GRASPS model could frame the task.”*

**6\. Check alignment**  
 *“Does my assessment plan actually measure the goals I identified in Stage 1? Identify misalignments and suggest adjustments.”*

# **Stage 3: Learning Plan**

### ***Learning Activities • Instructional Sequence • Scaffolding • Tech Integration***

**7\. Build a learning sequence**  
 *“Here is my performance task. What learning experiences would scaffold students toward success? Suggest a sequence including mini-lessons, practice, and technology-based explorations.”*

**8\. Differentiate with technology**  
 *“Given the needs of my learners (ELLs, IEPs, readiness levels), suggest differentiated activities or digital tools that still align with my Stage 1 goals.”*

**9\. Analyze tech integration**  
 *“Here’s my draft for Stage 3\. For each activity, identify the role of technology (SAMR) and propose manageable upgrades.”*

# **Whole-Unit Coherence**

### ***Ensuring the Stages Actually Work Together***

**10\. Check for coherence across Stages 1–3**  
 *“Here are all three stages of my unit. Identify places where the logic breaks down or where a student might be confused. What would Wiggins & McTighe flag as misalignment?”*

**11\. Offer tech-integration pathways**  
 *“Propose three different ways this unit could integrate technology—one minimal, one moderate, one transformative.”*

**12\. Redesign based on constraints**  
 *“Here are my school’s constraints (access, time, policies). Help me simplify or redesign the tech integration while keeping my core learning goals intact.”*

# **Iterate & Reflect**

### ***Refinement • Professional Judgment • Designer Mindset***

**13\. Push my thinking**  
 *“Identify two assumptions I seem to be making in this unit design and challenge them. How might I rethink the role of the teacher, student, or technology?”*

**14\. Warm/cool UbD critique**  
 *“Provide warm feedback (strengths) and cool feedback (areas to improve) on my overall unit design, based on UbD best practices.”*

**15\. Anticipate classroom realities**  
 *“Given my redesigned unit, what challenges might students encounter—technically, socially, academically? Suggest contingency plans or adjustments.”*
