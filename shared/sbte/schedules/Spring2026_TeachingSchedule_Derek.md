## RATIONALE FOR THIS SCHEDULE

### 1. **Safety & Weather Conditions**
- **Eliminated:** Monday & Wednesday morning commutes (RIT → Pine Brook → UofR = 70+ min drive)
- **Impact:** Reduces hazardous winter driving by 40% on those days
- **Benefit:** Afternoon teaching still occurs; only double commute is removed
- **Reality:** One hour of teaching ≠ 2+ hours of driving, especially in ice/snow

### 2. **Financial & Sustainability**
- **Reduced travel costs** on Mon/Wed (eliminated high-travel morning block)
- **Fuel savings:** ~40 miles/week not driving in winter
- **Wear on vehicle:** Reduced stress on car during hazardous conditions
- **Time cost:** Reclaim ~2+ hours/week for focused preparation

### 3. **Teaching Quality & Performance**
- **Better prepared lessons:** Afternoon prep time instead of morning rush
- **Mental clarity:** Less cognitive load from extended commutes
- **Student focus:** Can arrive refreshed vs. exhausted after driving
- **Reflection time:** Afternoon prep allows immediate post-lesson reflection
- **Compensatory strength:** Extended full-day teaching (Tue, Thu, Fri) increases observation of routines and continuous practice

### 4. **Balanced Academic-Placement Integration**
- **Maintains core teaching days:** Tue, Thu, Fri full days still provide 18+ hours/week
- **Accommodates coursework:** Afternoon blocks allow Monday/Wednesday morning RIT lectures
- **Reduces context-switching:** Fewer transitions between institutions per day
- **Allows course engagement:** Better participation in UofR seminars without exhaustion
